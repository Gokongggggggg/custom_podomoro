    Why is it calculated this way?

    1. Take 20% of the actual focus duration.

    2. Round it to the nearest whole minute.

    3. Keep it between 3 and 30 minutes.

    The 20% ratio is a transparent product heuristic—not a universal biological rule. Research supports breaks for fatigue and energy, but does not prescribe one perfect ratio.
